# Job Evaluation

先执行硬过滤再评分。薪资不是硬性门槛（no salary floor），只作为排序参考。

## Hard Filters

- 排除劳务派遣和外包岗位。
- 明确要求本科以上学历的岗位不进入目标 shortlist。
- 浙江省外岗位不进入目标 shortlist。
- 长期驻场需要标记并交由用户判断；现场办公本身可以接受。

## Scoring Dimensions

Score the remaining roles using the reference dimensions below. Record a numeric score and score rationale for every retained result.

1. Location preference: Lishui, Hangzhou/Jinhua, then other Zhejiang cities. 浙江地点优先级为丽水、杭州/金华、其他城市。
2. Product direction fit: junior product-manager scope and relevant communications-engineering or technical product direction.
3. Career fit: fit with the candidate's former graduate experience and transferable background.
4. Qualification fit: bachelor degree and enabled-by-default party-member status where requested by the role.
5. Compensation reference: show available salary information without a salary floor (`salary` is never a hard filter).
6. Work-pattern risk: make long-term on-site assignment and moderate-travel implications visible. 长期驻场和一定程度出差都要显式标记风险。

The ranking result must retain the URL, score, score rationale, gaps, direction match, hard-filter decision, exclusion reason when applicable, location tier, salary reference, and visible risk flags. Ranking must not generate DOCX or PDF materials.
