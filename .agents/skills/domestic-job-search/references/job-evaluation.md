# Job Evaluation

Use hard filters before scoring. Salary is never a hard filter.

## Hard Filters

- Exclude labor dispatch and outsourcing.
- Reject roles that explicitly require education above a bachelor degree.
- Keep roles outside Zhejiang out of the target shortlist.
- Flag long-term on-site assignment for user review; on-site work itself is allowed.

## Scoring Dimensions

Score the remaining roles using the reference dimensions below:

1. Location preference: Lishui, Hangzhou/Jinhua, then other Zhejiang cities.
2. Product direction fit: junior product-manager scope and relevant communications-engineering or technical product direction.
3. Career fit: fit with the candidate's former graduate experience and transferable background.
4. Qualification fit: bachelor degree and enabled-by-default party-member status where requested by the role.
5. Compensation reference: show available salary information without a salary floor.
6. Work-pattern risk: make long-term on-site assignment and moderate-travel implications visible.

The ranking result must retain score rationale, gaps, hard-filter decisions, location tier, salary reference, and visible risk flags.
